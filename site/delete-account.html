Delete your Letter Chain account

Developer: Meghraj Giri

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Letter Chain and sign in, if you aren't already.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Email meghrajgiri56@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Letter Chain account". We'll delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, including your name, email address and profile picture link
  • All your synced game results, scores, streaks, levels and badges

Nothing is kept, except copies in our hosting provider's backups, which are removed automatically within 30 days.

Played as a guest?

Guest progress is stored only on your device, and we hold no data about you. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
