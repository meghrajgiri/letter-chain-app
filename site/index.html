Two ways to play

Classic and Chain take turns as the daily puzzle, and you can play either one as much as you like.

  • Classic

    Find the hidden words in straight lines: across, down and diagonally. Swipe across a word, or tap its first and last letter.

  • Chain

    Every letter belongs to a word. Trace each one up, down or sideways until the whole grid is used.

  • Daily streaks

    One puzzle a day, the same for every player. Keep your streak going, and save it with a short video if you miss a day.

  • Play your way

    Play as a guest, or sign in with Google to keep your progress on every device. Hints when you're stuck, levels as you improve.
