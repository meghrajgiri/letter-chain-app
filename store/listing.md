## Full description (max 4,000)

```
Letter Chain is a calm, clever word puzzle with a fresh challenge every day.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: find the hidden words in straight lines, across, down, diagonally and, as you level up, backwards. Swipe across a word, or simply tap its first and last letter.
• Chain: every letter in the grid belongs to a hidden word. Trace each word through neighbouring letters, up, down or sideways, until the whole grid is used.

ONE DAILY PUZZLE FOR EVERYONE
• One daily puzzle a day, the same for every player around the world.
• Classic and Chain take turns, day by day.
• Themes are often inspired by what the world is talking about: sport, film, music, space and more.
• Keep your streak going and watch your week fill up.

PUZZLES THAT GROW WITH YOU
• Every puzzle has 4 to 6 words of different lengths.
• Pick Classic or Chain and we choose a fresh theme for you, from 33 themes such as Animals, Food, Football and Space.
• Classic and Chain each have their own level, and puzzles get harder as you level up.

EASY ON THE EYES, EASY TO PLAY
• Found words get a marker highlight, so crossing words stay readable.
• Letters that can't continue your word fade out, so you always know where to look.
• Stuck? A hint marks the first letter of a word, or reveal a whole word in Classic.
• Large letters, tap-to-select and TalkBack screen reader support.

YOUR PROGRESS, YOUR WAY
• Play as a guest. No account needed.
• Sign in with Google to keep your progress on every device.
• Track puzzles solved, words found, fastest times, streaks and badges.
• An optional daily reminder, at a time you choose.

Free to play and supported by ads. When you run out of hints, you can watch an optional video for one more.
```
